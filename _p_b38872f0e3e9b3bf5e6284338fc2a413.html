Business Services Intern 

The Consumers Credit Union team is looking to hire a passionate Business Services Intern to join our team at our corporate office in Kalamazoo. As an Intern, you will provide exceptional service to enhance member relationships and spend time getting to know about a career in your field.

Our ideal candidate has a great attitude, is genuine and passionate. You are purposeful and like to be the solution, but more importantly, you are a servant leader.

What you will do:

  • Assist with the department's annual review process in business services
  • Connecting with members and assisting with financial statement needs
  • Analyze financial statements
  • Performing credit pulls for members and performing cash flow and collateral analyses
  • Participate in mentor sessions, panels, and role specific training geared toward advancing your career
  • Work with a diverse team in one of the Best & Brightest Companies
  • Develop an individual development plan
  • Contribute to various potential projects such as:
    • Completing annual reviews
    • Stress testing large and/or troubled loans
    • Contacting members via phone and email to collect financial statements
    • Conducting market surveys
    • Exploring and presenting opportunities to penetrate new markets or product lines, such as USDA loans
    • Conducting prospect research

What you'll bring to the table:

  • College junior or equivalent
  • Ability to maintain confidentiality when handling sensitive information as well as following security guidelines
  • Communication Excellence – Exceptional written and verbal communication skills with professional rapport building ability.
  • Professional Presence – a polished appearance and composed demeanor that builds member confidence
  • Technical Proficiency - Knowledge of current technology, with the ability to easily navigate multiple software applications.

What you will gain:

  • Hands-on experience through a meaningful summer project and present your work to organizational leadership at the conclusion of the internship.
  • Gain exposure to commercial lending, prospect research, member relationship management, and market analysis within a collaborative business services environment.
  • Networking opportunities with professionals and other interns
  • Job shadowing experience outside of your department
  • College credit eligibility
  • Hands-on industry training
  • Mentorship from experienced team members
  • Volunteering Opportunities

What you didn't know about the team: 

At Consumers, you're joining a team that works closely together and counts on one another. You'll find the people here enjoy an unusual high level of camaraderie.

We are committed to Equal Employment Opportunity.

We are a proud Veteran & Military Friendly Employer.
