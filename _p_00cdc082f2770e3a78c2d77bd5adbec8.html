Supercharge Your Summer with Elire LLC.: Summer 2027 Technical Consulting Internship

About Elire:
Are you eager to infuse your summer with technical prowess and real-world software development experience? Elire LLC. invites you to join our Summer 2027 Technical Consulting Internship program. 

At Elire LLC., we are all about driving innovation, empowering clients, delivering success, and making an impact. As a mid-size software consulting firm specializing in Oracle Cloud ERP & HCM, PeopleSoft, Kyriba, and FIS systems, Elire is serving clients across diverse sectors, including Financials, Treasury, Supply Chain Management, and Human Resources/Human Capital Management were we are the bridge between technology and business excellence. 

Get ready to dive into innovative solutions, technical challenges, and meaningful impacts to the team, projects & enterprises we encounter. 

Internship Highlights: 

  • Full-Time & Paid: Earn while you learn with a full-time internship.
  • Technical Expertise: Work closely with our internal technical team, honing your computer science and software development skills.
  • Real-World Projects: Dive into active client projects and gain hands-on experience.
  • Varied Technologies: Explore a diverse tech stack, including Oracle Cloud ERP & HCM, PeopleSoft/PeopleTools, Kyriba, and FIS, and more.
  • Collaborative Environment: Collaborate with experienced professionals on the forefront of technical consulting.
  • Flexible Learning: Customize your experience by focusing on your areas of interest. 

Key Responsibilities: 

As a Technical Consulting Intern at Elire LLC., you will: 

  • Technical Project Support: Assist our internal technical team in implementing and optimizing enterprise systems.
  • Software Development: Contribute to the development and customization of solutions using cutting-edge technologies.
  • Problem Solving: Apply your analytical and programming skills to address technical challenges.
  • Collaboration: Work closely with team members to ensure project success.
  • Technical Documentation: Create and maintain technical documentation.
  • Testing: Participate in testing activities to ensure software and systems quality.
  • Innovation: Explore new technologies and solutions to drive innovation. 

Qualifications: 

To excel in this role, you should: 

  • Be currently enrolled in a computer science. Software development, MIS, networking or other related technical undergraduate or graduate program.
  • Understanding of basic technical databases, software’s, languages and networks
  • Possess hands-on experience with networking, support desk, software development, MIS; either through academic projects, internships or freelance work.
  • Demonstrate strong problem-solving skills and analytical thinking.
  • Be a proactive and collaborative team player.
  • Have a passion for technology and a desire to learn and adapt in a dynamic consulting environment.
  • Be legally eligible to work for any employer in the US.
  • Have excellent verbal and written communication skills in English. 

Application Details: 

Ready to supercharge your summer with technical consulting experience? Apply for the Elire LLC. Summer 2027 Technical Consulting Internship by submitting your application, resume and a short “cover letter” highlighting why you would be the perfect fit for this internship at www.elire.com/careers

Join the Elire Journey: 

Elire LLC. is committed to cultivating an inclusive and diverse workplace. We believe in the power of collaboration and innovation and welcome individuals who share our passion for excellence. Embark on your consulting journey with Elire LLC. and gain invaluable experience that will set you on a path to success in the dynamic world of enterprise systems consulting.

Over one hundred clients in the Public and Fortune 500 Financial, Services, and Utilities Sectors have benefited from Elire’s hands-on guidance and knowledge. We help achieve success by efficiently implementing, integrating, and upgrading software investments. We employ consultants with world-class skills and experience. To learn more, visit our website at www.elire.com
