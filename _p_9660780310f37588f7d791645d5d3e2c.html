Analytics Intern Opportunity

As an Analytics Intern, you’ll dive into the world of data, working alongside our talented analytics team to uncover trends, solve problems, and influence key business decisions. This is your chance to gain hands-on experience, learn from industry experts, and contribute to exciting projects that matter.

Internship Experience:

  • Orientation & Capstone – Kick off with a one-week orientation; wrap up with a presentation to executives.
  • Training & Shadowing – Gain hands-on exposure to claims and adjuster workflows
  • Intern Manager – Receive coaching, career feedback, and regular guidance.
  • GiveBack – Contribute to the community through volunteer opportunities.
  • Builders University & Events – Participate in professional development workshops and leadership roundtables.
  • Networking & Buddy Program – Build relationships with leaders, peers, and a dedicated intern buddy.
  • Hybrid Work – Work onsite in Raleigh, NC three days a week with remote flexibility the other two.
  • New Grad Opportunities – High-performing interns may be considered for full-time roles after graduation.

Skills to get the INTERNSHIP done RIGHT:

  • Current undergrad student working towards a degree in Statistics, Mathematics, Computer Science, Data Science, Data Analytics, or Economics not graduating prior to December 2027 or after December 2028.
  • You've got practical experience inside or outside of school or work.
  • Leader, you have demonstrable experience (group projects and/or clubs plus engaged in class)
  • Excellent communication skills, including written, verbal and interpersonal 
  • Proven team player who takes the initiative and works with others to achieve team goals
  • High-level analytical capabilities and a drive for critical thinking
  • Knowledge of SQL, R, or Python, and/or experience is a plus.
  • Proficient use of Microsoft Office (Excel, Word, and PowerPoint), as well as the desire and capability to learn new software or tools quickly
  • Ability to commit for 10 weeks (May 17 - July 23) during the summer of 2027, working 37.5 hours per week 
  • Willing to live or commute to Raleigh during your intern experience
