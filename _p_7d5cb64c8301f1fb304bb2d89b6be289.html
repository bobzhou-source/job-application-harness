Intern, Financial Planning and Analysis (Summer 2027)

RALEIGH, North Carolina

Apply

Description

At Martin Marietta, we are always looking for the best and the brightest, for people who have the potential to be the Company's future leaders. We are building on our foundation of success by selecting the finest people and helping them realize their potential. When you decide to build your career at Martin Marietta, you'll know what it's like to be respected, challenged, and rewarded.

As a Financial Planning andamp; Analysis (FPandamp;A) Intern, you will work alongside experienced professionals to support financial planning, forecasting, reporting, and analysis for Martin Marietta. This internship provides an opportunity to apply classroom knowledge to real-world financial and business challenges while gaining exposure to the financial planning process at a large public company.

Key Responsibilities

  • Follow and promote Martin Marietta's safety, environmental, and ethical standards in all work activities.
  • Actively participate in company initiatives and support a culture of operational excellence and continuous improvement.
  • Assist with financial planning, forecasting, budgeting, and reporting activities.
  • Support the annual planning process and gain exposure to financial planning activities across a large public company.
  • Assist with the preparation of financial reports, analyses, presentations, and other materials for divisional and corporate teams.
  • Participate in the preparation and analysis of information supporting quarterly 10-Q reporting and Earnings Release processes.
  • Assist with various ad hoc financial analysis and external audit requests.
  • Analyze financial and operational data to identify trends, variances, and opportunities for improvement.
  • Support the development and maintenance of financial models, reports, dashboards, and analytical tools.
  • Utilize tools such as IBM Cognos Analytics and Planning Analytics to support reporting and financial planning activities.
  • Utilize Alteryx and Tableau to create dashboards and automate data analytics processes.
  • Collaborate with divisional, corporate, and FPandamp;A teams to support business objectives and financial analysis.
  • Gain exposure to various Finance functions and core business operations throughout the organization.
  • Demonstrate initiative, professionalism, adaptability, and a strong work ethic while working independently and as part of a team.
  • Perform all responsibilities in accordance with the Company's Mission, Vision, Values, policies, procedures, and Code of Ethical Business Conduct.

Qualifications

  • Currently pursuing a Bachelor's degree in finance, accounting, business analytics, economics, or a closely related field
  • Minimum 3.0 GPA preferred.
  • Rising Junior or Senior preferred.
  • Financial planning, forecasting, or financial modeling knowledge is a plus.
  • Previous work experience related to finance, accounting, analytics, or business is a plus.
  • Strong analytical and inquisitive mindset with the ability to identify trends, ask questions, and solve problems.
  • Strong verbal and written communication skills with the ability to interact regularly with divisional, corporate, and FPandamp;A teams.
  • Strong Microsoft Excel and PowerPoint skills.
  • Ability to quickly learn new systems and demonstrate comfort using new technologies.
  • Ability to work independently with minimal supervision and effectively manage multiple priorities.
  • Ability to collaborate effectively within a team environment.
  • Growth mindset and ability to be highly effective in a fast-paced, dynamic work environment.
  • Must be available to work onsite at Martin Marietta's corporate office in Raleigh, NC; this position is not remote.

Why Martin Marietta?

At Martin Marietta, we invest in developing future leaders. Our FPandamp;A Internship provides meaningful project work, experienced mentors, and exposure to financial planning and analysis supporting one of North America's leading building materials companies.

Interns gain valuable industry experience while building relationships and skills that can lead to future internship opportunities and full-time career paths within Finance, FPandamp;A, Accounting, and other areas of the organization.

If you're looking for an opportunity to learn, grow, strengthen your financial and analytical skills, and gain hands-on experience supporting financial planning and business operations, we encourage you to apply.

A member of the S&P 500 Index, Martin Marietta is an American-based company and a leading supplier of heavy building materials – including aggregates, cement, ready-mixed concrete and asphalt. Through a network of operations spanning 28 states, Canada and the Bahamas, dedicated Martin Marietta teams supply the foundational resources upon which our communities thrive. Martin Marietta's Magnesia Specialties business produces high-purity magnesia and dolomitic lime products used worldwide in environmental, industrial, agricultural and specialty applications.
