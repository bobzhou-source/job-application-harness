Description

Altamira Technologies has a long and successful history providing innovative solutions throughout the U.S. National Security community. Headquartered in McLean, Virginia, Altamira serves the defense, intelligence and homeland security communities by focusing on creating innovative solutions leveraging common standards in architecture, data and security.

We focus on recruiting talented, self-motivated employees that research and develop both innovative and practical solutions that meet and exceed our customers’ data processing and intelligence extraction requirements.

Altamira is searching for creative and goal oriented summer interns to work alongside our experienced Software Development team to address real world national security requirements.

Desired Qualifications:

  • Familiarity with at least some of the following:
    • Programming Languages
      • C++
      • JavaScript / Typescript
      • Python
      • Java
      • HTML/CSS
      • Postgres/Postgis/general SQL
  • Working towards BS in computer science or computer engineering
  • Ability to work in a team setting and communicate with team members
  • Ability to learn new technologies and apply them to assigned work
  • *Must be a U.S. Citizen
